Source: `datasets\bigearthnet-txt\BigEarthNet.txt.parquet` (9,553,962 rows). Generated by `scripts/analyze_dataset.py` in 68 s.

## Contradictions with / additions to the brief

- Test has 115,753 images, not 116,835. The brief's figure equals test + bench (116,835): the 1,082 bench images are a separate set, not part of test.
- Adjacency: every train image has the binary `no` question (100.0%), but the binary `yes` and the MCQ exist only when the scene has a touching pair (79.1% of train images, not ~88%). Picking among the *available* items would make adjacency binaries ~2:1 `no`. The conversation builder therefore treats the group pick probabilities as absolute, and an unavailable pick skips the group.
- Relative position MCQ exists for 20.4% of train images (brief: ~21%).
- Bench is structured differently from train: several questions per category per image (e.g. up to 11 adjacency `yes` binaries), relative-position MCQs come in pairs (both directions), and season / climate / country MCQs exist for only 64.7% / 62.4% / 28.8% of bench images. Fine for single-turn eval. In train every image has exactly one of each.
- 2,449 area binaries use a zero threshold ('at least 0 m2', 'greater than or equal to 0%'). They are always `yes` and only test class presence. Kept: they are correct, and presence ordering already covers the leak.
